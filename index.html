This is a simplified model of the Kelana Jaya Line (Line 5), which runs driverless trains pushed by linear motors. Shapes and sizes are approximate and the loop is a toy track, with two real station names borrowed. The Ampang and Sri Petaling lines work differently: they have drivers on board and ordinary spinning motors that turn the wheels.

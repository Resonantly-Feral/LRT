A model of the Kelana Jaya Line (Line 5): all 37 stations in order, four-car trains at their real length and width, tunnels under the city centre. Every station sits at its real map position, with the whole map shrunk to about a third so a ride takes minutes. Between stations the track follows the line as drawn on OpenStreetMap, smoothed. The Ampang and Sri Petaling lines work differently: they have drivers on board and ordinary spinning motors that turn the wheels.

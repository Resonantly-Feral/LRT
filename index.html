A model of the Kelana Jaya Line (Line 5): all 37 stations in order, four-car trains at their real length and width, tunnels under the city centre. Every station sits at its real map position, with the whole map shrunk to about a third so a ride takes minutes. The buildings beside the line are the real ones from OpenStreetMap, as simple blocks, shrunk with the map while the trains stay full size. Between stations the track follows the line as drawn on OpenStreetMap, smoothed. The Ampang and Sri Petaling lines work differently: they have drivers on board and ordinary spinning motors that turn the wheels.
